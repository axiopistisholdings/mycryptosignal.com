2. Cryptocurrency Trading Risks

2.1 Market Volatility

Cryptocurrency markets are extremely volatile and unpredictable:

  • Prices can fluctuate 20-50% or more in a single day
  • Markets operate 24/7 with no circuit breakers
  • Flash crashes and pumps occur without warning
  • Regulatory news can cause instant price movements
  • Manipulation and wash trading are common

2.2 Loss of Capital

YOU CAN LOSE YOUR ENTIRE INVESTMENT.

  • Many cryptocurrencies have gone to zero
  • Projects can be abandoned, hacked, or exposed as scams
  • Exchange failures can result in total loss of funds
  • Smart contract bugs can drain liquidity
  • Rug pulls and exit scams are frequent

2.3 Leverage and Liquidation

If you trade with leverage (borrowed funds):

  • You can lose MORE than your initial investment
  • Liquidations can happen in seconds
  • You may owe money to exchanges
  • Cascading liquidations can cause market crashes

2.4 Regulatory Uncertainty

  • Cryptocurrency regulations are evolving and unclear
  • Governments may ban, restrict, or heavily regulate crypto
  • Tax treatment varies by jurisdiction and is complex
  • Legal status of tokens can change overnight
  • Exchanges and services may be forced to close or restrict access
